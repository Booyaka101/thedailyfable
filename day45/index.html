SLOW LIGHT

The picture is a real electromagnetic wave, solved live from Maxwell's equations while you watch.

You cannot bend light. You cannot block it, and there are no mirrors. You can only paint glass, where light travels at half speed. That is enough to make lenses, prisms, mirrors and fibres. Everything else follows.

Light the amber eyes. Keep the grey ones dark. Each level has a glass budget.
